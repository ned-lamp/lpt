13 - Installation of MetaMask and Study Spending Ether per Transaction
=====================================================================

Aim: Installation of MetaMask and study spending Ether per transaction.

KEY TERMS
---------
MetaMask        : a crypto wallet as a browser extension; lets you hold Ether and
                  connect to Ethereum apps (like Remix).
Wallet          : stores your private keys and shows your balance (the coins
                  are on the blockchain, not in the wallet).
Ethereum        : a blockchain that supports smart contracts; its coin is Ether (ETH).
Transaction     : sending Ether or calling a smart contract; recorded on the blockchain.
Gas             : units of work a transaction needs (a simple ETH transfer = 21,000 gas).
Gas price       : price per gas unit, in Gwei (1 Gwei = 10^-9 ETH).
Transaction fee : Gas used x Gas price  (this is the Ether "spent" per transaction).
Base fee        : minimum fee set by the network (burned).
Priority fee    : tip to the validator to process your transaction faster.
Test network    : (Sepolia) a practice Ethereum network with free fake Ether.
Faucet          : a website that gives free test Ether.

STEPS - INSTALLATION
--------------------
1. Open Chrome and go to https://metamask.io -> click "Download".
2. Click "Add to Chrome" -> "Add extension".
3. Pin the MetaMask fox icon from the extensions (puzzle) menu.
4. Open MetaMask -> click "Create a new wallet" -> agree to terms.
5. Set a password (used to unlock MetaMask on this computer).
6. Secure your wallet: write down the 12-word Secret Recovery Phrase on paper.
   Never share it with anyone. Confirm the words when asked.
7. Wallet is ready - you will see "Account 1" with 0 ETH and an address (0x...).

STEPS - GET TEST ETHER
----------------------
8. Click the network dropdown (top-left) -> turn ON "Show test networks".
9. Select "Sepolia" test network.
10. Copy your account address (click on the account name).
11. Open a Sepolia faucet (search "Sepolia faucet", e.g. Google Cloud / Alchemy),
    paste the address and request test ETH. Wait a minute, balance updates.

STEPS - STUDY SPENDING ETHER PER TRANSACTION
--------------------------------------------
12. In MetaMask create a second account: account menu -> "Add account".
13. Switch back to Account 1 -> click "Send".
14. Select Account 2 (or paste its address) -> enter amount, e.g. 0.01 ETH -> Next.
15. Before confirming, note the fee screen:
      - Estimated gas fee
      - Max fee
      - Total = amount + gas fee
    Click "Edit" / "Market" to see Low / Market / Aggressive fee options.
16. Click "Confirm". Status goes Pending -> Confirmed.
17. Open the transaction in the Activity tab -> "View on block explorer" (Etherscan).
    Note: Transaction Hash, Block, From, To, Value, Gas Limit, Gas Used,
          Gas Price, Transaction Fee.
18. Check: Account 1 balance decreased by (amount + fee); Account 2 increased
    by amount only. The difference = Ether spent on the transaction.
19. Repeat with a different fee option (Low / Aggressive) and compare the fee
    and confirmation time.

CONCLUSION
----------
MetaMask was installed and a wallet was created. Ether spent per transaction
equals gas used x gas price; a simple transfer uses 21,000 gas, and a higher
gas price gives faster confirmation but costs more.

VIVA QUESTIONS
--------------
- What is MetaMask?            -> Browser wallet to store ETH and use dApps.
- What is a Secret Recovery Phrase? -> 12 words that can restore your wallet.
- Why gas fee?                 -> Pays validators for processing and prevents spam.
- Gas used for a simple transfer? -> 21,000.
- Gwei vs Wei?                 -> 1 ETH = 10^9 Gwei = 10^18 Wei.
